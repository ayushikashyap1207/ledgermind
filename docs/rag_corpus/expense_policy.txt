LedgerMind Employee Expense Policy 2026

Section 1. Documentation
Travel and client entertainment expenses must include original itemized receipts. Card statement screenshots are not considered original receipts.

Section 2. Submission and Approval SLA
Employees should submit expense reports within 7 calendar days. Managers are expected to approve or reject submitted reports within 3 business days. Approved expense reports are reimbursed within 10 business days.

Section 3. Non-reimbursable Items
Personal commuting, parking tickets, and first-class upgrades are not reimbursable unless pre-approved by HR and Finance.
